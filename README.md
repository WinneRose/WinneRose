### Glad to see you here!  
I'm a multidisciplinary product designer and developer working across product strategy, interface design, full-stack development, and game development. I enjoy taking ideas that are still unclear, shaping them into usable systems, and building the first version people can actually experience.

In the software side, I work primarily with Next.js, Node.js, and TypeScript on the frontend and backend, while also developing AI/ML models and mobile applications. I actively follow the latest technologies through GitHub and Product Hunt to integrate them into my work.

In the design aspect, I create modern, minimal, and intentional designs with a focus on typography and user experience. I get inspired through platforms like Dribbble and Behance to continuously evolve my design perspective.
